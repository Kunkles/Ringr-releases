# Ringr

A Mac app for making Preston iris marking rings. Type the millimetre
positions you measured, and it prints the ring straight to a Brother
P-touch — numerals turned a quarter turn, ticks on the edge, cut lines at
both ends.

It also prints the ruler you measure against, which is the point: both
strips come off the same printer at the same scale, so whatever the feed
error is, it lands on each of them equally and cancels out.

**Downloads are on the [Releases](../../releases) page.** The source is
private; this repo is here to hold the builds and the changelog.

## What you need

- A Mac running macOS 14 or later
- A Brother P-touch that speaks the raster protocol — developed against a
  **PT-D610BT** over Bluetooth
- **18 mm TZe tape.** Not a preference. The layout, the numeral sizes and
  the printable band are all worked out for 18 mm.

## Installing

Unzip, drag **Ringr** to your Applications folder, open it. macOS will
ask for Bluetooth the first time; that is the printer.

## Two things worth knowing before you spend tape

**Tooth pitch.** A ring you marked against one brand of motor will read a
little off against another, even though both are driven from the same
Preston MDR. They more or less work — but if a ring feels half a stop out
on a different motor, that is why, and it is worth noting which motor a
ring was measured with. There is a field for it.

**Intermediate marks are measured, never worked out.** Thirds are not
evenly spaced between whole stops on a real lens, and the app will not
pretend otherwise. If you want a mark, measure it.

A tip from the walkthrough: make one permanent ruler ring, mark your
stops on it in Sharpie, then draw over them in dry-erase when you are
done and lift the lot with the felt eraser.

## Trademarks

Ringr ships a handful of lens manufacturers' logos so you can put one on
a ring. Those marks belong to their owners and are included only so the
ring says whose glass it is. No affiliation or endorsement.
